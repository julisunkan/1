APP: NDA Generator
ROUTE: /ndagen

DESCRIPTION:
NDA Generator is an AI-powered legal document tool that drafts professional, jurisdiction-aware Non-Disclosure Agreements in seconds. Enter the parties, purpose, duration, and jurisdiction — and receive a legally sound NDA ready for review and e-signature. Sign digitally, download as a polished PDF, and store all agreements in one place.

FEATURES:
- AI-generated NDA content tailored to your specific use case
- Jurisdiction-aware clauses (customizable by country/state)
- Configurable disclosure duration and agreement scope
- Built-in e-signature support for both disclosing and receiving parties
- Signature pad with touch and mouse support
- One-click PDF download with embedded signatures and wax-seal styling
- Signed agreement history with searchable saved NDAs
- "Fully Executed" banner automatically applied after both parties sign
- Secure document storage with unique document IDs
